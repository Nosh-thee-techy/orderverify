# What OrderVerify can't catch

A well-made fake message, with a code that looks real and a date that matches that code, cannot be caught from the text alone. The words can be typed by anyone.

Upload your M-Pesa statement when you want a stronger check. If the receipt is not on the statement as money in, the order is held. That is the check the text cannot do.

OrderVerify does not call Safaricom. It cannot see your live balance. It does not know if a till number in a chat is yours. It does not decide that a person is dishonest. It shows what does not match, and you decide whether to dispatch.

Night-time payments are normal. The rules do not flag them.

A friend or a spouse may pay for the buyer. A different name is a reason to look, not a reason by itself to refuse the order.
